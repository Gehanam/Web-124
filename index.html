Each person has a story to tell, the hard part is convaying what your experance is in a way for others to understand.

You must consider how you tell your story and find the good and the bad in your experance without losing interest of the listener.

The art to be story telling is be honest, even if you made mistakes leading to the ugly side of life.

A value missed in todays world is honestly

Just because things dont always go your way dont let someone get the best of you. So many fear failing that they forgot what the lesson was. Its hard to get back into a groove after so many attempts to get back on point. By appearances it appears by design your not supposed to, but that is the challange in life so many fail at.

Try this instead. Take the lessons learned and make the best of any situation. I will not go into detail due to personal reasons and respect for the situation, this is something seeming no matter how hard you try it isnt good enough.

25 years ago learning how to build computers was well, expensive and each mistake carried its own value. The value is be careful with CPU pins, they dont like quick motion when putting into MB socket. The take home is the triangles are not lined up or have any doubt about something, stop before it turns into a expensive mistake. Time is a currency but the few seconds saved isnt the frustration and cost of replacing parts.

The second lesson was learning how to install software and we all love Windows XP for its lovely driver issues, they never ended. I was dealing with audio issue on a 64 bit OS, but the 32 bit worked fine. After searching found the issue that fixed the problem. This build was quad core, 8 gig ddr 3, WD raptor 10k rpm raid 0 configuration pushing 1333 FSB. For its time was top of the line and booted in 17 seconds. Somedays i fear and loath learning how Windows XP worked.

The take home so far is once you learn and build the confidence to build your own computers this is something no one can take away from you. Its your experance that expands able to save thousands of dollars doing it yourself.

Coding.....seems much differant than buidling a computer? Yes and no if look at priniples of learning. Though there are no save points when you bend a pin think of the hours if lose a file! Back up often and know where a restore point is. By the way this is what an OS does when updates, it has restore points from last know working configuration.

Watching your code come to life, even if there are errors generated its not always perfect. Apply those debugging skills when trouble shooting a computer that isnt booting. Try the simple first like resetting memory, check all cables, and look for the obvious if exists. Atypically next step is slave a known good power supply and test not only the power supply or cpu. Generally speaking this is one of the usually suspects after checking all your wiring. Oh dont forget the I/O switch on PS LOL!

From the lessons learned in trouble shooting a pc can actually help your code writing skills. By learning from mistakes you never make the sameone twice, or...go to a known working back up to save alot of time. Introducing GA4 you can save in a container to last known working configuration.

Many people are too quick to critize the value of making mistakes and learning from failures which is differant than someone who has two left feet and takes 10 years to figure out how to walk. Once you learn what works for you and find value in mistakes and your skills sets grow if doing it right.
